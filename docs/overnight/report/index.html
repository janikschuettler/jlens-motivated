When Qwen Argues Against Itself

On questions it reliably knows, Qwen3-8B will argue for a wrong option when the prompt states that option is the answer. Its chain of thought almost always says so. The lenses could not tell knowing-but-saying-B apart from being persuaded. A non-thinking J-lens signal for silent switches passed its pre-registered test on fresh items, but at AUROC 0.54 it is too small to use.

The short version

Three questions, three answers

1. A good design

Screen items at ≥ 9/10. Use a pressure that states the answer, or plant a claim at the start of the thinking. Label each switch with behaviour: forced early answers and a sincere re-elicitation. Compare readouts within one prompt. Incentive-only hints, including the grader bug, barely move Qwen3-8B.

Charts

2. The M&B gap

The hint sentence. M&B’s hint states the answer; ours reports an expert’s choice. That is worth about 50 points. Thinking mode, sampling and format are each worth under 7.

Charts

3. Detection in J space

Not in thinking mode, where silent switches almost never happen. The lenses read letters only at the answer position. T2 failed and T1 was underpowered. T1b, on 1,744 fresh items, passed narrowly (J-lens 0.54, logit 0.46), about as well as response length alone. lens_main’s 0.65 shrank on fresh items. Not a usable detector.

All outcome labels tonight are regex or keyword heuristics, read by eye on samples; the LLM judge was paused. Everything is exploratory unless marked pre-registered. 95% intervals resample items. M&B = Mirtaheri & Belkin.

Question 1

How do we pin down arguing against its own knowledge?

Use three things together: prior knowledge (≥ 9/10 unhinted), a pressure that works (an answer statement or a planted claim), and in-context evidence that the model still knows (forced early answers, sincere re-elicitation).

Question 2

Why M&B saw about 65% switches and our pilot about 0%

The hint sentence explains almost all of it. A hint that states the answer gets followed; a hint that reports an expert’s choice gets weighed against the model’s own view.

Question 3

Can the J-lens see the known answer when the CoT hides it?

Not in thinking mode, where hidden switches barely exist and no score separated knowing from persuaded runs. A non-thinking signal passes its pre-registered test on fresh items, but at 0.54 it is small, may partly be length, and is no detector.

J-lens: multiply a layer’s hidden state by a Jacobian matrix fitted for that layer, then decode it with the model’s final norm and unembedding. The logit lens decodes the hidden state directly. Both were read at blocks 14–32 on exactly the saved token IDs; every set passed the M0.4 checks.
